This lesson content explains the concept and benefits of Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) in improving the accuracy and relevance of large language models (LLMs).

Challenges with LLMs
* LLMs generate text based on training data but can provide outdated or unsupported answers without sources.
* They may confidently give incorrect information and lack the ability to update knowledge without retraining.

How RAG Works
* RAG combines LLMs with a content retrieval system that fetches relevant, up-to-date information from a data store.
* The LLM uses this retrieved content along with the user query to generate a grounded and evidence-backed response.

Benefits of RAG
* Keeps answers current by updating the content store instead of retraining the model.
* Provides sources for answers, reducing hallucinations and increasing trustworthiness.
* Enables the model to admit when it does not know an answer if the data store lacks relevant information.

Overall, RAG enhances LLMs by integrating retrieval mechanisms to produce more accurate, reliable, and context-aware responses.
